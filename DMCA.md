# Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) Notice & Takedown Policy

The **Dongnime** project complies with 17 U.S.C. § 512 and the Digital Millennium Copyright Act ("DMCA"). It is our policy to respond to any infringement notices and take appropriate actions under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act and other applicable intellectual property laws.

---

## 1. Nature of This Project

1. **Tool, Not a Content Host**: Dongnime is an open-source project providing modular source extensions for compatible Android media reader/player applications (such as Aniyomi and Anikku).
2. **No Media Stored**: Neither this repository, its maintainers, nor its infrastructure host, stream, store, cache, or transmit any video files, media streams, images, or copyrighted material.
3. **Public Parser**: The extensions provided herein function strictly as specialized HTTP clients that parse publicly accessible HTML/DOM documents and JSON APIs on the third-party websites they interface with, similar in nature to a standard web browser.
4. **Independent Operation**: Dongnime is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with any of the third-party websites or content providers indexed by its extensions.

---

## 2. Notice and Takedown Procedure

If you are a copyright owner, an authorized agent thereof, or an authorized representative of a website indexed by an extension, and you wish to request the removal of a specific extension scraper module from this repository:

Please submit a written notification containing:

1. **Identification of the Extension**: The specific extension module name (e.g., Anichin, AnimeXin, Nekopoi) and the target website URL.
2. **Proof of Authority**: A statement establishing that you are the copyright holder or are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
3. **Contact Information**: Your name, organization/company, address, telephone number, and official email address.
4. **Good Faith Statement**: A statement that you have a good faith belief that the scraping or indexing of the website interface is unauthorized.
5. **Accuracy Statement**: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, made under penalty of perjury.

### Submission Channel:
- **Via GitHub Issue Tracker**: Open a formal issue titled `[DMCA/Takedown] <Extension Name>` on our [Issue Tracker](https://github.com/dongnime/extensions/issues).

---

## 3. Response and Resolution

Upon receipt of a valid removal notice:
- Maintainers will acknowledge receipt within **24 to 48 hours**.
- The requested extension module will be promptly **removed** from the source repository, its build artifacts deleted from release distribution channels, and its entry removed from `index.min.json`.
